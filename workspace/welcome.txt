This is your assistant's sandbox folder.

Try asking it things like:
- "what files are in my workspace?"
- "read welcome.txt"
- "make a file called todo.txt with my 3 top priorities for this week"
- "rename todo.txt to this-week.txt"

Once you trust it, set ASSISTANT_WORKSPACE in .env to a real folder (like
your Downloads directory) and it'll operate on that instead of this one.
